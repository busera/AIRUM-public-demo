# AIRUM Public Demo Product Brief

## Purpose

AIRUM Public Demo is a reduced public-facing showcase for the AI Risk Universe Matrix concept. It should help an auditor, researcher, educator, or collaboration partner understand the method shape without exposing the private implementation.

## Primary audiences

- Internal Audit practitioners evaluating how AIRUM could strengthen AI audit discovery preparation.
- Researchers and educators assessing the concept for teaching or empirical validation.
- Conference, event, or collaboration contacts who need a clear public link.

## Core promise

AIRUM prepares a better first AI audit discovery conversation. It does not automate audit judgment or publish the full methodology.

## Public boundary

The demo may show:
- Conceptual AIRUM workflow.
- A reduced source-backed risk subset.
- Example candidate discovery-preparation output.
- Public narrative about deterministic, reviewable scoping.

The demo must not show:
- Full AIRUM working risk counts.
- Complete source mappings.
- Internal scoring or selection logic.
- Private audit, employer, or client material.
- Operational implementation details that would allow reconstruction of the private method.

## Success criteria

- The public boundary is visible before detailed methodology claims.
- The page feels like a practitioner artifact, not generic AI/SaaS marketing.
- Navigation and controls work on narrow screens and touch devices.
- The demo remains static, low-maintenance, and easy to review before publication.
- Future design QA can use this file plus DESIGN.md as context.
