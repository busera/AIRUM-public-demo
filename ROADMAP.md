# AIRUM public roadmap

Near-term public roadmap:

1. Publish the explanatory project repository.
2. Link the repository from the university 2-pager and the Medium article.
3. Add a reduced interactive demo only after separate publication review.
4. Keep the demo focused on two purposes:
   - AI Risk Universe exploration.
   - Deterministic Risk Scoping for pre-discovery preparation.
5. Keep the full working implementation, internal scoring, private mappings, and operational build details out of the public repository.
